## 🚜 Problem Statement
Indian farmers largely rely on traditional methods, limiting productivity, profitability, and sustainability. This highlights the **need for accessible, modern solutions** to empower them.

---

## 📊 Market Overview
The global smart agriculture market is expected to grow from **$16.2 billion in 2023** to **$25.4 billion by 2028**, at a CAGR of 9.4%.

---

## 🌟 Features of Kisaan Mitra

### 1. **Crop Health Monitoring**
   - AI-powered crop health analysis through leaf image scanning.
   - Customized pesticide suggestions for optimal yields.
   - Connect with experts for personalized guidance.

### 2. **Soil Testing Services**
   - On-site soil sample collection.
   - Suggestions for best crops based on soil, weather, and market value.
   - Awareness of government soil testing schemes.

### 3. **Water Level Adjuster**
   - Solar-powered IoT device for automated water supply control.
   - Parameters analyzed: soil type, season, crop type, and field area.

### 4. **Customized Products**
   - **IoT Devices**: For efficient water and soil management.
   - **Fertilizers & Pesticides**: Tailored to specific crop health needs.
   - **Seeds**: Personalized for maximum growth and profitability.
