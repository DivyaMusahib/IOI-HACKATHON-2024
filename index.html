OUR SERVICES

We provide the perfect service for you.

Soil Health Management

We analyze soil samples to provide precise insights into soil health, helping you optimize nutrients and improve crop productivity.

Automatic Irrigation

Our solar-powered automatic irrigation system adjusts watering based on land area, soil condition, crop type, and weather, ensuring efficient and sustainable water use for optimal crop growth.

Climate-Smart Farming & Crop Diversification Recommendations

Our platform offers climate-smart farming and crop diversification recommendations tailored to weather patterns, soil type, current market prices, and demand, helping you make informed decisions for maximum profitability and sustainability.

Soil-test

Get an in-depth report on your soil's health and expert guidance to ensure your crops thrive and stay disease-free.


IoT Device

Optimize irrigation with our IoT device that monitors weather, crop type, and soil quality, automating water supply for efficient and timely irrigation

Crop-Scanner

Using advanced image recognition, our system analyzes leaf images to detect potential crop diseases, connecting you to experts for timely solutions and care.
